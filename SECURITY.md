# Security Policy

Tatvarth Systems LLP takes the security of its software seriously. Thank you for helping keep our
products and the people who use them safe.

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Do not open a public issue, pull request, or discussion for a security problem.**

Report it privately by either route:

- Email [contact@tatvarthsystems.com](mailto:contact@tatvarthsystems.com) with the subject line
  `Security report`.
- Where a repository has it enabled, use **Security → Report a vulnerability** to open a private
  GitHub security advisory.

Please include:

- What is affected (product, URL, or repository) and its version if known.
- Steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept.
- The impact as you understand it.

## What to expect

- We acknowledge reports within **3 business days**.
- We aim to confirm and triage within **10 business days** and keep you updated until it is fixed.
- We credit reporters who want credit once a fix has shipped.

## Scope

In scope: the software and websites published by Tatvarth Systems LLP and its repositories.

Out of scope: social engineering of staff, physical attacks, denial-of-service or volumetric
testing, and third-party services we use (report those to the vendor).

## Safe harbor

If you act in good faith, avoid privacy violations and service disruption, and report to us before
any public disclosure, we will not pursue legal action against you for your research.
