# Code of Conduct

We want participation in Tatvarth Systems projects to be respectful, honest, and useful to
everyone, regardless of background or experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback; keep it about the work, not the person.
- Be honest about what you know and do not know.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Spam, trolling, or deliberately disruptive conduct.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation that violates this code.
Report concerns to [contact@tatvarthsystems.com](mailto:contact@tatvarthsystems.com). Reports are
handled privately.
